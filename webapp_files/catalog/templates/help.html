Help and Getting Started

Explore the map and navigate

Open the map to explore bike routes and locations around Harrisonburg, Virginia. Solid lines are main routes; dashed subroutes use their main route's color. Use the map controls or touch gestures to zoom and pan. Select a named waypoint marker to see its name. Select a route line to see its name and comfort rating.

Start a route

  1. On the map, select the route line you want to follow.
  2. Select Go in the route popup.
  3. Allow your browser to use your location. Navigation uses GPS to show your position and keep the map centered on you.
  4. At connections between routes, the app may show route choices with simple cues such as Continue straight, Turn left, or Turn right. Select a choice to switch to that route. When no junction choices are shown, stay on the selected route.
  5. Select End route when you are finished.

The navigation banner shows the selected route's comfort rating and your distance from the nearest mapped route. That distance is not distance to a destination or the next turn.

Find and compare routes

  1. Open Routes.
  2. Enter a search term and choose whether it matches route names, colors, or both. If neither Name nor Color is checked, both are searched.
  3. Optionally select Official to show official routes only, or choose a comfort rating from 1 to 5 to show routes rated at least that high. A higher rating means more comfortable; a displayed rating of 0 means no rating has been set. Unrated routes are left out when a comfort rating is selected. Select the chosen rating again or use Clear to remove filters.
  4. Select Search to apply your choices.

Select a route's Comments link to open its details, including waypoints and route information. The detail page also links to feedback, export, and (for routes you imported) update and delete actions.

Import, export, and manage routes

Import a route

  1. Sign in; importing requires an account. Open Routes and select Import a route.
  2. Draw a line on geojson.io and choose Save, then GeoJSON. Upload the resulting .geojson or .json file.
  3. If the file does not specify a route name or supported color, enter a name or choose a color on the import form.

Files can be up to 1 MB. Route lines need at least two valid points, written in GeoJSON order (longitude, latitude), and must stay within {{ area_name }}. Supported route colors are red, yellow, black, purple, blue, green, orange, pink, maroon, and indigo. The importer accepts GeoJSON line features and the project's waypoints.json format; non-line features such as points and polygons are skipped. A file with multiple line features can import multiple routes.

Imported routes belong to the account that uploaded them and are only visible to that account. After importing, find them in Routes.

Export or copy route data

Open a route's detail page and select Download as GeoJSON. You can export any route visible to you. The browser saves a .geojson file containing the route as JSON. The app does not display the raw JSON or provide a copy button; to copy it, open the downloaded file in a text editor. You can upload the file again or open it in geojson.io.

Update or delete an imported route

On the detail page for a route you imported, select Update with a new file to replace its line. An update file must contain one route line; existing feedback stays with the route.

Select Delete route and confirm to permanently delete your imported route. Any subroutes and feedback attached to it are deleted too. Official routes and routes imported by other accounts cannot be changed or deleted by you.

Share route feedback

Open Feedback or a route's detail page, choose the route, and submit a comfort rating from 1 (very uncomfortable) to 5 (very comfortable) with optional comments. You need to sign in to submit feedback. Your profile shows recent feedback and links to your full feedback list, where you can manage your submissions.

Find locations

Open Locations and select a name to see its coordinates and the routes that pass through it. Named waypoint markers on the map also show their names when selected.

Accounts and installation

You can browse routes without signing in. Sign in to import, update, or delete your own routes and to submit feedback. If you do not have an account, use Sign up.

Your profile shows your recent feedback, previous rides, saved routes, and marked locations when available. Choose Edit profile to change your username, comfort level, or the Show my location on the map setting, which controls whether your location is shown automatically while browsing the map. Choose Change password to update your password. Navigation still needs browser location permission.

To install BikeWay on a compatible device, use Install BikeWay on the welcome page. On iPhone or iPad, open the site in Safari, choose Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Troubleshooting and limitations

  • The map may ask to use your location when it opens or when you start navigation. If your location does not appear, enable location services on your device and allow this site to use location in your browser settings, then reload the map. You can still browse routes without sharing your location.
  • If map tiles do not load, check your internet connection and try again. Map tiles come from external map providers.
  • If an import is rejected, check the form's error message. Common causes are an invalid JSON file, unsupported color, too many bytes, missing route line, invalid coordinate order, or points outside the mapped area.
  • The Plan a trip page is not yet an origin-and-destination planner. To follow an existing route, use Map, select its line, and choose Go.
  • Map navigation is basic GPS guidance, not full turn-by-turn navigation: it does not calculate a trip from an arbitrary start to an end, give spoken directions, or automatically reroute you. GPS accuracy and map data can vary. Check your surroundings and do not interact with your device while riding.
