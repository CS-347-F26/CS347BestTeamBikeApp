Help and Getting Started

Learn how to explore Bike Transit and find a route that works for you.

  • Explore the map
  • Find and compare routes
  • Share route feedback
  • Accounts and installation

Explore the map

Open the map to see routes and locations. Solid lines are main routes; dashed lines are subroutes. Use the map controls or touch gestures to zoom and pan. Select a named waypoint marker to see its name.

Find and compare routes

  1. Open Routes and enter a search term.
  2. Choose whether the term should match route names, colors, or both. If neither field is selected, both are searched.
  3. Optionally select Official to show official routes only, or choose a comfort rating from 1 to 5 to show routes rated at least that high. Select the chosen rating again to clear it.
  4. Select Search to apply your choices, or Clear to show all routes again.

Select a route's Comments link to view its details and waypoints. The detail page also links to the route feedback form.

Share route feedback

Open Feedback, choose a route, and submit your comfort rating and optional comments. You need to sign in to submit feedback; create an account first if you don't have one.

Accounts and installation

Your profile shows your feedback and any saved routes, previous rides, or marked locations associated with your account. Use Edit profile or Change password from the profile page to manage your account.

To install BikeWay on a compatible device, use Install BikeWay on the welcome page. On iPhone or iPad, open the site in Safari, choose Share, then Add to Home Screen.
